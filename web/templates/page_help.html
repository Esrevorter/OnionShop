The short version

  1. Prices are shown in your currency (USD / EUR / AUD), but payment is always in Monero (XMR).
  2. Funds are held in escrow until you confirm receipt, or the inspection window ends.
  3. Every order has its own payment address and an exact amount. Never reuse an old address.
  4. Delivery details are encrypted to the vendor's PGP key. The site cannot read them.
  5. Everything is in-app: order updates arrive in Messages. No email, no tracking scripts.
