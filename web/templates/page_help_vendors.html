Vendor Guide

← Help Center

1 · Becoming a vendor

  1. Apply from Apply to become a vendor with your shop name.
  2. Pay the registration fee at Vendor Fee (amount set by the operator, entered in USD, paid in XMR). A unique address is generated; payment is detected automatically.
  3. An administrator then approves your shop. Both steps are required: paying alone does not activate the shop, and approval alone does not unlock selling until the fee is settled.

2 · Required setup before you can sell

  • PGP public key — upload it in Account. Buyer delivery details are encrypted to this key. Without a key, buyers cannot check out with your items. Keep the private key offline; the server never sees it. See the PGP tool.
  • Shipping options — define at least one active option in Shipping (title, price, delivery estimate in days). Listings cannot be created without it, and the estimate drives the buyer's auto-release deadline.
  • Ships-from — set your dispatch region in Account so buyers see it.

3 · Listings

  1. Create listings in My Listings: title, description, price in USD, stock, category, images.
  2. Images are re-encoded and stripped of metadata (EXIF/GPS) automatically.
  3. Listings start as draft; press Publish to make them visible. Remove them any time.
  4. Stock is decremented atomically at checkout — you cannot oversell.

4 · Handling orders

  1. You are notified in Messages only when an order is paid and in escrow.
  2. Open the order, copy the PGP-encrypted delivery block, and decrypt it offline with your private key.
  3. Ship, then press Mark as Shipped (add an optional note for the buyer). This starts the inspection window: shipping estimate + 3-day grace.
  4. The buyer confirms receipt, or funds auto-release when the window ends. Open disputes block auto-release until a moderator resolves them.
  5. If you do not ship within 3 days of payment, the order is auto-refunded to the buyer.

5 · Getting paid

  • Wallet shows your available balance (released orders) and pending (in escrow / shipped / disputed), plus ledger history.
  • Request a withdrawal to your XMR address. Requests are reviewed by an administrator; when marked paid, the payout is broadcast on-chain and recorded against your balance.
  • Balances come from the double-entry ledger, not from the chain — the ledger is the source of truth.

6 · Reputation

Statistics shows sales, customers, ratings and listing health. Buyers can review shipped/released orders; your real dispatch speed is computed from order history and shown publicly.

OpSec: never put personal details in listings or messages, never reuse a payout address across identities, and keep your private keys off this server.
