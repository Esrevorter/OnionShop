08/14/2026 - Announcement for Vendors - Dont let the phishers trick you

Dear Vendors,

We would like to remind all vendors to stay alert and careful.

Recently, there have been multiple cases where phishers contact vendors with phished accounts while pretending to be legitimate users of the platform.
They try to trick the vendor by claiming they have lost their PIN and then asking them to "buy" from them, so the vendor will withdraw the coins and split the money, making it sound like a quick and easy deal.
They usually present the situation as normal and urgent, aiming to gain trust and pressure the vendor into responding quickly.

We have already identified, warned and banned several vendors involved in such activities.
Banned accounts will also no longer be unbanned, as it is suspicious, if a user has several accounts and has forgotten the pin of all of them and exploits the vendors to withdraw the coins.

Please note:
- Any suspicious message should be treated with caution.
- If we identify a vendor involved in such activities, their account will be permanently suspended.
- If you receive any suspicious messages or requests, report them to us immediately.

Your cooperation is essential to keep our platform safe.
