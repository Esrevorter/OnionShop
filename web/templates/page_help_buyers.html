Buyer Guide

← Help Center

1 · Account & safety

  1. Register with a username and a long password (10+ characters). A CAPTCHA keeps bots out.
  2. Set your anti-phishing phrase in Account. It appears in the header on every page — if it is missing or wrong, you are not on the real site.
  3. Optional but recommended: enable two-factor authentication (TOTP) and save your backup codes.
  4. Always verify the .onion address before signing in.

2 · Finding things

Browse the Marketplace, use Search, or open a vendor's shop. Prices display in your chosen currency — switch with the USD / EUR / AUD pills. Settlement is always in XMR.

Listings show real dispatch history when available ("2d dispatch"), not just the vendor's claim.

3 · Checkout

  1. Add items to your Cart. Items are grouped per vendor.
  2. At checkout, choose a shipping method for each vendor.
  3. Enter your delivery address / instructions. These are encrypted to the vendor's PGP public key — the server stores only ciphertext and never decrypts it.
  4. Enter a refund XMR address. If anything goes wrong before shipment, your money returns here automatically.
  5. Solve the CAPTCHA and place the order. One checkout with several vendors becomes one order per vendor, each with its own payment address.

4 · Paying

  1. Open the order and press Pay. Click the address block to select it, then copy.
  2. Send exactly the shown XMR amount to that address. Amounts are rounded to 6 decimals so what you see is what you send.
  3. Payment is detected automatically after the required confirmations — usually within a minute or two of the network confirming.
  4. Underpaid? Send the remainder to the same address. Overpaid? The order is held for manual review — do not send more.
  5. Unpaid orders expire after 12 hours. Nothing is charged.
Never reuse an address. Each order has a unique subaddress; sending to an old one cannot be matched to a new order.

5 · After payment

  • in_escrow — funds are held. The vendor is asked to ship. You can still Cancel & refund at this stage.
  • shipped — inspect the goods. Press Release Payment when satisfied, or Extend escrow once (+7 days) if you need more time.
  • Auto-release — if you do nothing, funds release after the shipping estimate plus a 3-day grace period.
  • Problem? Open a dispute from the order. A moderator resolves it: release to vendor, or refund to your refund address.
  • If a vendor never ships, an automatic refund is issued after 3 days in escrow.

6 · Messages, reviews, support

  • Every order event lands in Messages. The header link turns red while you have unread messages.
  • You can hide a conversation from your inbox, restore it, or delete your own messages.
  • Leave a review once an order is shipped or released (within the review window).
  • Account or platform problems: open a support ticket.

FAQ

Why Monero and not Bitcoin?

Bitcoin payments are permanently linkable on a public ledger. Monero keeps your purchases private. This shop never asks for your real identity.

Can the site read my delivery address?

No. It is encrypted to the vendor's public key before storage. Only the vendor's private key can decrypt it, offline.

Where do refunds go?

On-chain, to the refund address you supplied at checkout.

Why no JavaScript?

Scripts are disabled by policy for safety. Everything works with plain forms and links, including copy-to-select address blocks.
